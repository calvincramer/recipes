## Ingredients
- spices (all ground or powder)
    - 1 tsp turmeric
    - 1 tsp cardamom
    - 1 tsp cumin
    - 1 tsp cinnamon
    - 1 tsp cloves
    - 1 tsp garlic powder
    - 1 tsp nutmeg
    - 1 tsp garam masala
    - 1 tsp black pepper
    - 1 tsp ginger powder
    - 1 tsp onion powder
    - 1 tsp chili powder (opt for heat, adjust as you wish)
    - 1 tsp curry powder
- 150 g butter
- 150 g all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp honey
## Steps
- roast all spices in non-stick pan on low heat for 5 minutes. Set aside
- butter to pan, melt. Add honey
- add flower, stir to combine
- fry roux around 15 minutes until desired darkness achieved. Stir often
- turn heat off, add spices back in, combine thoroughly
- let cool a little
- add to containers lined with cling wrap
- make indentations / cuts to aid in breaking apart later
- let cool down more, then put in fridge. Transfer to freezer as desired.
## Notes
- it makes a good curry.
