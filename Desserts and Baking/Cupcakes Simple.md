## Ingredients
- dry
    - flour 280g - 2 1/3 cups
    - sugar 400g - 2 cups
    - baking powder 1 tbsp
    - salt 3/4 tsp
- eggs 2
- milk 360g - 1 1/2 cup
- vegetable oil 109g - 1/2 cup
- vanilla extract 2 tsp
## Steps
1. preheat oven to 325F
2. combine dry ingredients. Sift the flour. Mix
3. Whisk the eggs separately until smooth
4. wet -> separate bowl mix the milk, oil, and vanilla
5. add in wet to dry, mix
6. add in eggs, mix
7. put in muffin cups
8. bake 20-25 minutes, until toothpick comes out clean
9. cool outside oven for a few minutes, then transfer to wire rack to cool completely
## Notes
- too sweet and wet. Try reducing sugar and wet ingredients
