Makes 16 cupcakes
## Ingredients
Dry:
- 420g (3.5 cups) all-purpose flour
- 4 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt

Wet:
- 267g (1 1/3 cup) granulated sugar
- 300g (1 1/4 cup) milk
- 240g (1 cup) sour cream
- 114g (1/2 cup) butter, melted
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp lemon zest

- 1.5 cup blueberries, frozen
## Steps
1. preheat oven to 350F
2. put muffin liners in muffin tin
3. sift together all dry ingredients, mix
4. in food processor mix sugar and lemon zest to get flavor out of zest
5. another bowl add all wet ingredients (not the blueberries) until smooth
6. fold in wet to dry, don't over-mix
7. put in blueberries without mixing too much
8. fill muffin cups 3/4 full
9. bake 30-35 minutes
## Notes
- nice to add lemon juice / powdered sugar glaze on them
- this recipe needs more lemony taste in the actual muffin
