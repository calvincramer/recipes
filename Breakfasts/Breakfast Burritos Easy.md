Makes 12 burritos
## Ingredients
- 375g (3 cups) frozen potato tots or hash browns
- 12 eggs
- 60g milk
- 500g (1 lb) breakfast sausage / Italian sausage
- 250g (2 cups) cheese shredded - cheddar
- 12 8-inch flour tortillas
- 1 bell pepper, diced small
- 1 onion, diced small
- cilantro, chopped so not too big or long stems
## Steps
- bake / fry potatoes until extra crispy
- eggs, milk, salt, pepper, chicken bullion, a little sugar in bowl. Mix very thoroughly
- fry sausage and break it up into smaller pieces. Cook until done. Get some crispy pieces. When done remove to fine mesh strainer to drain oil
- with oil from before cook bell pepper and onions until good, then add eggs. Cook until done. Then take out to the bowl with sausage
- add cheese and potatoes and cilantro to bowl
- gently mix
- cut out 12 pieces of aluminum foil, about 8 inches square
- warm the tortillas
- make burritos, placing each on aluminum foil square, then wrap in foil
- freeze if desired
## Notes
- bake in oven (in aluminum foil) 400F 10-12 minutes if not frozen, 25-30 minutes if frozen
- or microwave frozen (remove foil) 2 minutes
