## Ingredients
- 4x eggs
- spinach, torn roughly by hand
## Steps
- in bowl crack eggs. Add salt, pepper, chicken bullion powder, a little garlic powder, a little sugar
- mix thoroughly until no more strands of eggs left
- add in spinach
- heat non-stick pan until hot
- add butter, melt, then add egg mixture. Reduce heat to medium low
- once set on bottom and almost done: optionally add cheese or other fillings inside on one side. Then fold in half
- cook to finish. Flip as necessary
