## Ingredients
- 4 cloves garlic minced
- 2 inch ginger minced or grated
- 1 tbsp fresh turmeric minced, sub 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1-2 serrano peppers diced (opt for heat)
- spices
    - 1 tsp ground cumin
    - 1/2 tsp ground coriander
    - 1/2 tsp Indian red chili powder, sub 1 tsp chili powder
    - 2 tsp curry powder
    - 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 cup (180-190 g) red lentils
- 2 cups (480 ml) broth (vegetable)
- 1 14-oz can crushed tomatoes
- 1 14-oz can full-fat coconut milk
- 3 tbsp creamy almond butter
- fresh cilantro
- lemon juice
## Steps
1. rinse lentils until water runs clear. Watch out for rocks
2. heat deep skillet, add 1 tbsp neutral oil, once hot add garlic, ginger, fresh turmeric if using, serrano pepper. Cook for 2 minutes
3. add in spices (and ground turmeric if using) and salt and pepper to taste. Cook until fragrant.
4. add broth, deglaze
5. add lentils and tomatoes, mix well
6. simmer covered 20-25 minutes until lentils cooked through
7. add in coconut milk, almond butter
8. salt and pepper to taste
9. cook a few minutes uncovered until thickness to your liking
10. turn off heat, add in some lemon juice and cilantro
11. serve with rice and naan
