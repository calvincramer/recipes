## Ingredients
- 4x hass avacodos
- 1/2 white or red onion
- 1x serrano pepper or 2x jalapeno (to taste)
- a couple limes, or just lime juice
- cilantro, chopped fine
- 1x plum tomato, diced. Only keep firm flesh no liquid parts

## Steps
- small dice onion
- small dice pepper
- finely chop cilantro
- dice avacado
- in bowl (or molcajete or big mortar and pestle) put half of the onion, pepper, half of cilantro, some coarse salt, some lime juice in. Pound / grind until fairly broken down
- add in avacados, mix/grind until desired consistency
- salt to taste
- lime juice to taste
- add in rest of onion and cilantro
