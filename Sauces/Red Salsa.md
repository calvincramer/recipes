## Ingredients
- 6x roma tomatoes, sliced into quarters
- 1/2 red onion, sliced into 3 pieces
- 6x cloves garlic whole
- cilantro
- 1 jalapeno
- 2 tsp chicken bullion powder
## Steps
1. reserve 1/3 of the half onion to dice very small, added in at the end for texture. The rest of the onion keep big slice
2. char the tomatoes, garlic, onion, jalapeno. Then put these in a food processor molcajete. Blender also may work. Get to desired consistency.
3. Add in cilantro, the small dice red onion
4. Add in chicken bullion powder
5. lime and salt to taste
