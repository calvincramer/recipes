## Try 1 - James Beard
- dough:
    - 540g (4.5 cups) all purpose white flour
    - 25g (2 tbsp) sugar
    - 12g (2 tsp) salt
    - 367 (1.5 cups) whole milk, warm
    - 9g (1 tbsp) active dry yeast
    - 1 tsp baking powder
- some olive oil
- 144g (1 cup) hulled sesame seeds
- 1-2 tbsp grape molasses (sub date molasses or honey)

1. put everything for the dough in mixer with dough hook
2. mix on medium speed until dough comes together in a soft and pliable ball. Add milk if too hard
3. rub dough with oil, cover bowl 1 hour or doubled in size
4. sesame coating -> large shallow bowl combine sesame seeds with grape molasses and 1 tbsp water. Mix. Add water as necessary until have wet mixture not too sticky that it clumps and not too thin
5. after dough risen punch down air bubbles, divide into 6
6. roll and stretch each piece in a log 8 to 12 inches long, attached the ends in a circle
7. rest for 15 minutes
8. dip dough rings in sesame coating, stretch into final shape "0", set aside to rest around 10 minutes
9. bake 450F for 15-20 minutes, deep golden
10. cool on wire rack

### Thoughts
- try using 30% less sesame seeds since there were lots extra
- try adding twists when making into circle
- measure stretching it out. It needs to be stretched out quiet a bit
- took 19 minutes to bake the top and 22 on bottom. I didn't switch halfway through but you should.
